Overtime / Half-Day planner — standard day = 8 machining hrs Trigger T13

Station runs 4h instead of 8h that day — capacity is halved, not added
Hourly OT and Half Day are separate, independent controls — only one applies to a station on a given day (ticking one clears the other). Half Day means the station is only available for 4 of its standard 8 hours that day (e.g. planned shutdown, power cut, partial shift) — capacity for the day is halved, not added, so load % rises accordingly and that day's Worker efficiency and Supervisor efficiency (weighted) figures are computed against the reduced 4h baseline, not the full 8h day — see Reports.
